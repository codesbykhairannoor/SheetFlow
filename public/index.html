Operational Model

How SheetFlow processes your data

A transparent three-step pipeline running entirely inside your Canva session.

01

Provide Sheet URL

Paste the sharing link of your public or published Google Sheet. SheetFlow extracts the Document ID and accesses the live CSV export directly via Google endpoints.

02

Automatic Column Parsing

SheetFlow identifies your header row and generates dynamic field tags for each column, such as Name, Date, Clinic, or Status, ready for one-click insertion.

03

Direct Canvas Synchronization

Step through your records with the row navigator. Each step pushes data directly into Canva text layers using the official Canva Apps SDK APIs.

Zero-Server Guarantee

Your data never touches a proprietary database

SheetFlow was built with privacy by design. When you synchronize a Google Sheet, data travels directly between Google's servers and your local Canva editor browser tab.

Read Full Privacy Policy
  • ✓ Zero Persistence: We operate no remote databases or storage buckets that retain your spreadsheet content.
  • ✓ Session Scope: Data lives in browser RAM during your active session and is wiped when you close the Canva editor tab.
  • ✓ No Commercial Profiling: We do not track, scrape, or sell business records, email lists, or customer names.
  • ✓ Official SDK Compliance: Fully compliant with Canva Developer Terms and Google API Limited Use disclosure standards.
