## 3. Full App Description (For Canva App Store Detail Page)

```markdown
Turn your spreadsheets into a high-speed design engine.

SheetFlow connects your Google Sheets directly to Canva, empowering you to populate, preview, and batch-update text, prices, badges, and images in seconds. No more tedious copy-pasting or dealing with messy CSV files.

### ⚡ Key Features

* **Instant Live Sync**: Connect any Google Sheet via sharing link or Google OAuth. Updates flow directly to your canvas in real-time.
* **Smart Auto-Matching**: Automatically detects and matches canvas text boxes to spreadsheet columns using column headers, sample values, or prefix labels.
* **Dynamic Template Tags**: Insert `{{ColumnName}}` tags into your templates. Swap out entire cards, prices, and descriptions with one click.
* **1-Click Multi-Element Update**: Select your entire design (`Ctrl + A`) and hit **"Update All in Design"** to refresh every linked element simultaneously.
* **Seamless Row Navigator**: Cycle through rows of product catalogs, event attendees, or student certificates with forward and backward controls.
* **Durable Storage**: Powered by resilient IndexedDB persistence—your active dataset and recent spreadsheets stay saved even if you reload Canva.

---

### 🎯 Ideal For

* **E-Commerce & Flash Sales**: Update product titles, original prices, sale prices, stock badges, and image links instantly.
* **Certificates & Diplomas**: Batch-generate hundreds of personalized certificates with attendee names and dates.
* **Real Estate & Property Flyers**: Populate property addresses, prices, agent contacts, and feature bullet points.
* **Social Media Content**: Automate quote cards, carousel slides, daily tips, and event announcements.
* **Event Badges & Name Tags**: Create custom badges for conferences, webinars, and team events in minutes.

---

### 🚀 How It Works (3 Easy Steps)

1. **Connect**: Open SheetFlow in the Canva sidebar and paste your Google Sheet link (or sign in with Google).
2. **Design**: Build your layout or insert dynamic template tags like `{{Name}}` or `{{Price}}`.
3. **Flow**: Step through your rows and click **"Update All in Design"** to populate your artwork instantaneously!
```
